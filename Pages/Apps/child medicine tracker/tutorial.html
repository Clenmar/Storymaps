What the walkthrough covers

🔑Signing inEach family has its own code and its own private log. Ask Clenmar for one.
💊Adding a medicineCopy the amount from the label; the app builds every dose time and warns about the 24-hour limit.
✓Marking a dose givenOne tap, or 🕐 to set the real time if you are catching up. Both phones see it.
🍼Nappies, temperature and weightOne tap per nappy, and charts that show whether a medicine is helping.
📧RemindersAn email when a dose is due, and a calendar you can subscribe to on the iPhone.
🩺The doctor's reportA tidy summary and detail sheet to hand over at an appointment.

A short guided walkthrough of the Medicine Tracker. The on-screen phone is a faithful recreation of the app, narrated for tutorial purposes.

Medicine Tracker only keeps time. It never works out how much medicine to give — always follow the label or your doctor, nurse or pharmacist.
