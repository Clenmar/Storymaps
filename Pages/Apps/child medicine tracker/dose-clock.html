    ❓ How Dose Clock works
    • Add each medicine once with the amount from the label and how often it repeats. Dose Clock builds every dose time.
    • Tap Given when the medicine is actually in. It records the time and who gave it, so the other parent can see.
    • The timeline shows every medicine on its own row. Drag it sideways, pinch to zoom, tap a dose to mark it given, skip it or undo.
    • Safety checks warn you if a dose is early, if the 24 hour limit is reached, or if two medicines share the same active ingredient.
    • Fever line — log temperatures and the timeline draws them over the doses, so you can see whether the medicine is helping.

    Please read. Dose Clock only keeps time. It does not decide how much medicine to give, it does not know your child's weight or history, and it is not medical advice. Use the amount printed on the label or given to you by your doctor, nurse or pharmacist. Two medicines can share the same active ingredient, so check before giving both. If your child is under three months old, is getting worse, will not drink, or you are unsure about anything, call your doctor or a nurse line. In an emergency call 911.

    Never give a dose based on a reminder alone. Check the label, the amount and the child every time.

    Without a family code everything stays on this device only. With a family code the schedule is stored in your family's private repository so both parents' phones agree, and reminder emails can be sent.
