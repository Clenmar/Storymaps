    👨‍👩‍👦 Sign in so both parents see the same log

    Type your family code on each phone. Every medicine, dose, nappy, temperature and weight is then shared between you both — whoever gives the dose, the other one sees it. The server also sends the reminder emails on its own, even with the app closed.

    Admin — church families

    Advanced — server address (admin only)

    Who gets the emails

    📅 Getting the doses into the iPhone calendar

    Tap Add to calendar on any medicine (or Add all to calendar below the list) and pick the way that suits the phone:

    1. Google Calendar — opens the Google Calendar app or site with the whole course already filled in as one repeating event. Tap Save and it appears in the iPhone calendar too, as long as the Google account is added under Settings → Calendar → Accounts.
    2. Open in Calendar (iPhone) — hands the schedule to iOS through the share sheet. Choose Calendar, then Add All. If iOS shows a preview first, tap the file and then Add All.
    3. Download the .ics file — best on a computer or Android. On iPhone the file lands in Files, so use one of the two options above instead.

    Every dose becomes an event with an alert at dose time and a heads-up 5 minutes before. Pop-up alerts — tap 🔔 at the top of this page; those fire while the page is open.

    ❓ How Medicine Tracker works
    • Add each medicine once with the amount from the label and how often it repeats. Medicine Tracker builds every dose time.
    • Tap Given when the medicine is actually in. It records the time and who gave it, so the other parent can see.
    • Given earlier? Tap 🕐 next to any dose (or on the Now card) and set the real time — quick chips for "30 minutes ago", or the exact clock time, and who gave it. The same 🕐 on a dose already recorded lets you correct the time. The Baby log has a When box for catching up on a nappy or a reading from earlier.
    • The timeline shows every medicine on its own row. Drag it sideways, pinch to zoom, tap a dose to mark it given, skip it or undo.
    • Safety checks warn you if a dose is early, if the 24 hour limit is reached, or if two medicines share the same active ingredient.
    • Fever line — log temperatures and the timeline draws them over the doses, so you can see whether the medicine is helping.
    • Nappies and weight — tap 💧 or 💩 once each change; the app counts the day, charts the week and warns you if wet nappies drop off, which is the first sign of not drinking enough. Weigh-ins draw a trend line.
    • Both parents, one log — sign in with your family code on each phone. The record lives in the church's private repository, not on the phones, and changes merge: Mum marking a dose and Dad logging a nappy at the same moment both survive.
    • Today at a glance — the strip on the Now tab puts every medicine, nappy, temperature and weight of the last 6 and next 18 hours on one line, with the next dose ringed. Tap any dose to mark it given.

    Please read. Medicine Tracker only keeps time. It does not decide how much medicine to give, it does not know your child's weight or history, and it is not medical advice. Use the amount printed on the label or given to you by your doctor, nurse or pharmacist. Two medicines can share the same active ingredient, so check before giving both. If your child is under three months old, is getting worse, will not drink, or you are unsure about anything, call your doctor or a nurse line. In an emergency call 911.

    Never give a dose based on a reminder alone. Check the label, the amount and the child every time.
